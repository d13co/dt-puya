### Added

- Added dt-algorand's time-travel ops (AVM 13, app mode) as `algopy.op.TimeTravel`: `seek` (`tt_seek`) moves a program's reads to a past round, and `acct_change`, `holding_change`, `local_change`, `asset_change`, `app_change`, `box_change`, `global_key_change_*`, `local_key_change_*` and `box_prefix_change` (`tt_*_change`) find when state changed. They come from `langspec.timetravel.json`, and only dt-algorand's POST /v2/query runs them: simulate and blocks refuse a program with them.

### Fixed

- In a subroutine that may seek, one that runs `tt_seek` or calls one that may, the optimizer keeps state and box reads, `global Round` and `FirstValidTime` on the side of a `tt_seek` they were written on, and doesn't reuse a read, or a box's known existence, from before a seek after it. Other subroutines compile as before.
- Asserting a box's exists flag read before a `box_del`, or before a call to a subroutine that isn't pure, no longer lets the optimizer drop a later assert that the box exists.
